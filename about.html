Early Life and Background

Born in 2004, my interest in computer science grew from a simple curiosity about how software works under the hood. Throughout my studies, I have focused heavily on core fundamentals like data structures, algorithms, and memory management. Instead of just jumping into high-level frameworks, I spend my time practicing algorithmic problem-solving and building a strong, practical foundation in how systems actually operate.

Career & Technical Execution

I am currently focused on backend development and systems programming. I enjoy building projects that require custom data structures and direct database integration, primarily working with C++ and MySQL. My day-to-day involves solving algorithmic challenges and applying those concepts to hands-on projects, like building in-memory caches. Right now, my main goal is to write clean, efficient code and develop a practical understanding of how reliable software is built.
